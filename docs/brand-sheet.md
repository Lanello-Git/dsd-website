# DSD Dakwerken, brand sheet

Derived from the existing logo (gold roofline icon and serif wordmark on black). Peter said the colours of the old site were his favourite part, so the site keeps gold and black as the identity.

## Palette

| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Ink | #141414 | Header top bar, footer, dark sections, headings |
| Gold | #b8974f | Primary action (buttons), trust bar, accents. Sampled from the logo |
| Gold deep | #86682b | Text links, eyebrows, hover states (5.4:1 on white) |
| Gold pale | #f3ead6 | Icon badges, light accents |
| Cream | #f8f5ef | Alternating section background |
| White | #ffffff | Page background, cards |
| Line | #e6e0d4 | Borders |
| Roof red | #a9442d | Required-field marks only; the photos bring the red |

## Type

- Display: DM Serif Display (matches the serif wordmark, confident, legible). H1 to H3.
- Body: Source Sans 3 (variable), 400/600/700. Body, buttons, labels, H4.
- Scale: 13 / 15 / 17 / 20 / 24 / 28-36 / 34-48 / 36-62 px (fluid with clamp). Line height 1.6 body, 1.12 headings.
- Fonts are self-hosted (fontsource), no Google Fonts request, GDPR-safe.

## Spacing and shape

- Spacing scale: 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48, 72, 96 px.
- Radius: 6 px buttons and inputs, 12 px cards, 20 px photos.
- Shadows: soft, warm-grey, two levels.
- Container: 74 rem.

## Icons

Lucide line icons, inlined as SVG in `src/components/Icon.astro`, 2 px stroke. No emojis anywhere.

## Signature element

The gold trust bar directly under every hero: four facts (30+ jaar, 10 jaar garantie, eigen ploeg, 150+ projecten). Everything around it stays quiet (white, cream, black) so the gold band is the thing people remember.

## Voice

Belgian Dutch, formal "u", first person plural "wij", Peter named where it builds trust. Plain, warm, concrete. No em dashes, no exclamation marks in body copy.
