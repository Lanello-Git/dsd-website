## 2. Voice and language

- **Belgian Dutch (Vlaams), formal "u"**, never "je/jij". Company voice is "wij/ons" (a team), with Peter named where it builds trust.
- Plain, confident, warm, local. Talk like a competent contractor explaining things at the kitchen table. Short sentences. Specific beats clever.
- Flemish words, not Dutch-from-the-Netherlands: dakwerker (not dakdekker), premie (not subsidie, except in the phrase "premies en subsidies" once), stelling (not steiger), omgevingsvergunning (not bouwvergunning), rijwoning/rijhuis, halfopen bebouwing, open bebouwing, fermette, hoeve, villa, Gyproc, plaatsbezoek, offerte, btw, EPDM, dakpannen, leien, onderdak, isolatie, sarking, Rd-waarde, EPC, verreiker, luifel, hemelwater, regenwaterafvoer.
- Never write "welkom bij". Never write "wij zijn dé specialist". Never use "hoogwaardig" more than once per page.
- **No em dashes (the long dash). Ever.** Use a comma, a period, a colon, parentheses or a plain hyphen. This is checked automatically and the page is rejected if one appears.
- **No emojis.** No exclamation marks in body copy (one in a CTA heading is the maximum).
- **No fabricated testimonials, reviews, ratings, employee counts, awards or certificates.**
- Numbers: write "10 jaar", "150+ projecten", "30 km". Prices as "vanaf 150 euro per m²" or "tussen 55 en 90 euro per m²". Use "euro" in running text, "€" in tables.

## 4. Belgian facts you may state (2026)

- **6% btw**: for renovation work on a private home that is at least 10 years old, when the contractor delivers and places the materials and invoices both together. Otherwise 21%. DSD handles the required declaration/attest.
- **Mijn VerbouwPremie (Vlaanderen)**: a premie for dakisolatie is possible, the amount depends on the owner's income category and the rules change regularly (since 1 March 2026 the lower income categories no longer get the dakisolatie premie for owner-occupied homes). Conditions: work by a contractor, insulation with Rd-waarde of at least 4,5 m²K/W, home at least 15 years old, application via vlaanderen.be. DSD provides the contractor attest and invoice details. Always advise to check the current conditions on vlaanderen.be.
- **Omgevingsvergunning**: a roof renovation that keeps the same shape and volume is usually free of a permit (vrijgesteld); a dakraam usually too. Changing the roof shape, adding a dakkapel, or working on a protected building (for example in the Brugse binnenstad) can require a permit or advice from Onroerend Erfgoed. Say "meestal" and offer to check with the gemeente.
- **Dakisolatie is verplicht** in Vlaanderen for existing homes (dakisolatienorm since 2020, minimum Rd 0,75 m²K/W, part of the woningkwaliteitsnormen); and since 2023 buyers of a home with EPC label E or F must renovate to at least label D within 5 years (renovatieverplichting). You may mention both briefly.
- Weather: West and East Flanders get a lot of wind and rain from the coast; storm damage, moss on north-facing roofs, and clogged gutters are common problems.

## 5. Page structure and SEO rules

- One H1 per page containing the target keyword and (for city pages) the city. Title tag: keyword + place + "| DSD Dakwerken", max 60 characters. Meta description 140 to 160 characters with a benefit and a call to action ("Vraag een gratis offerte aan").
- Question-shaped H2s where natural ("Wat kost een dakrenovatie in Aalter?").
- FAQ: 6 to 8 real questions in the customer's own words (cost, timing, premie, btw, vergunning, garantie, region, emergency). The **first sentence of every answer answers the question directly**; then add detail. Answers are 2 to 4 sentences.
- Each page links to at least 2 other pages inside the body copy (not only nav or footer): service pages link to related services and city pages; city pages link to service pages and nearby city pages.
- City pages must be genuinely local: name deelgemeenten and wijken, the typical housing stock (rijhuizen, villa's, fermettes, hoeves, appartementen), local roof problems, heritage rules where relevant, distance to Aalter. Under 20% text overlap with other city pages. Never a find-and-replace of the city name.
- Service pages must explain: the customer's problem and the benefit, what is included, how it is done, what it costs (table), why DSD, where we do it, FAQ, CTA.
- Image alt text is written in Dutch, describes the photo, and mentions the place or service where natural.
